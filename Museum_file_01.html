    View in Your Space

    Place the artifact in your surroundings

    See at real-world scale. You decide when to start; nothing is turned on until you choose a mode below.

    1. Choose a mode that your device supports.
    2. Allow camera access only if you pick camera mode — your browser will ask first.
    3. Move slowly so the app can find a surface, then tap to place the object.
    4. Drag to rotate, use the size control, or reset and try again.
